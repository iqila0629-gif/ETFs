Nordea 1 - European High Yield Bond Fund, comment July
European high-yield spreads tightened modestly in July, closing at 287 bps, although higher underlying government bond yields more than offset the spread compression, resulting in a negative return for the broader asset class. Default rates remained low and broadly stable at 1.9% based on JP Morgan data, while issuance slowed to €9.6 billion, marking the weakest July volume since 2023 after the exceptionally strong supply seen in May and June. The Nordea 1 – European High Yield Bond Fund (BI EUR) returned -0.24%, outperforming its benchmark (-0.41%). Performance was supported by security selection in Services, Telecommunications and Financial Services, with positive contributions from Virgin Media O2 and the absence of exposure to Aston Martin and CoreWeave. Detractors were limited, with the absence of Victoria PLC representing the only meaningful drag. The Fund remains conservatively positioned, maintaining its focus on Insurance, Services and senior secured single B exposures, while continuing to emphasize downside protection and selective alpha generation.
